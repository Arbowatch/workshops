Introduction to R for Public Health Surveillance

Introduces R for reproducible data handling, analysis, and visualization.
Published

October 27, 2025

Introduction

R and RStudio are powerful, open-source tools widely used for modern public health data analysis, enabling analysts to manage, explore, visualize, and communicate data-driven insights effectively. This module introduces participants to the R programming environment and the RStudio interface, with a focus on practical applications in public health surveillance and epidemiological analysis.

Using the tidyverse workflow, participants will learn essential data analysis processes, including importing, cleaning, transforming, summarizing, and visualizing health data. The module provides hands-on experience with key R packages such as readr, dplyr, tidyr, and ggplot2 for efficient and reproducible analytical workflows.

By the end of the module, participants will be able to apply R to perform descriptive analyses, generate meaningful visualizations, interpret disease patterns and trends, and create reproducible reports that support evidence-based public health decision-making.

TipModule Focus

Develop practical R skills for managing surveillance datasets, calculating key public health indicators, creating informative visualizations, and producing reproducible analytical outputs for routine public health analysis.

Prerequisites

Before attending, please ensure you have:

  • Completed Workshop 01
  • Installed the sf package in R: install.packages("sf")
  • Reviewed the field safety guidelines (see Materials below)
  • Brought sturdy footwear and waterproof field sheets
